Cranfield Ultimate

Cranfield Ultimate was established in 2022 by Gregor Arbuckle (Glasgow Ultimate), Huw Whitworth (Fire of London, Muntjacs), Qingqing Hua (Muntjacs, 3302) and Divyansh Sachan (Curve, Zoo). Closely linked with OUU (Open University Ultimate). Enjoy!
